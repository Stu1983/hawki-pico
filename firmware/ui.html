On your phone

An icon on your home screen
iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome's menu, Add to Home screen. It opens this page full screen; sign in with your PIN once there.
A widget showing the oil left (iPhone, iPad)
  1. Install the free Scriptable app.
  2. Tap Copy the widget script below.
  3. In Scriptable, tap +, paste, and tap the title to name it Oil. Tap ▶ and allow it to find devices on your local network.
  4. Press and hold your home screen, tap Edit, Add Widget, pick Scriptable and add it. Press and hold the new widget, Edit Widget, and choose Oil as its Script.
  5. Lock screen too: press and hold it, Customize, and add Scriptable the same way.
Show it
It shows the oil left, % full and the order-by date, and opens this page when tapped. iOS updates it now and then (usually every 15 to 30 minutes) while your phone is on your home Wi-Fi; away from home it shows the last figures it had.

Oil used in a typical year
Optional. It stands in for last year until you have one of your own: each month is forecast from it, scaled by how you are running against it so far, and it is the average year on the history page. A month you have real readings for goes by those from then on, never mixed with this. Leave it empty if you don't know.

Oil prices

…
 
Northern Ireland only. Once a day, the cheapest and average price for 900 L from cheapestoil.co.uk, for your postcode district: the first half of your postcode, like BT51. Leave it blank for the Northern Ireland average.

Home Assistant

…
 
Sends the readings to Home Assistant over MQTT, and Home Assistant finds each tank by itself. In Home Assistant: install the Mosquitto broker add-on, make a user for the monitor (Settings → People → Users), then enter its details here.

Security

…
 
Lets someone with this Pico's maintenance token (it's in config.json, readable over USB) upload files and run code over Wi-Fi. Leave it off unless you're fixing something.

Backup

A backup holds every tank's settings, deliveries (supplier and price) and all saved readings. It doesn't include the Wi-Fi password. Importing one onto this or a new Pico replaces those tanks' settings and deliveries and merges the readings.
